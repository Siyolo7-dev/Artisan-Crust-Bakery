Our Story

Tucked in a cosy corner of leafy Rondebosch, you’ll find CRUST bakery, fledgling business of owner and patisserie chef Jamie Tucker.
What started as a small home-baking venture in Gqeberha in 2020 has become a local favourite in Cape Town for pastries, breakfast and coffee.
It’s been an epic journey, and we can’t wait for what’s next



CRUST LOVES LOCAL

WHY CRUST BAKERY

What makes us different

Plenty of places sell cake. Here is why Cape Town keeps coming back to us.

Instant, honest pricing

Most cakes are priced the moment you build them. No waiting days for a quote, no surprises at collection.

Baked fresh to order

Nothing sits in a freezer waiting. Every cake is made for your date, so it arrives at its absolute best.

Something for everyone

Gluten free, vegan, nut free and banting options across most of our range, made properly, not as an afterthought.

Delivered and set up

Collect from us or let us deliver across Pretoria and Johannesburg. Wedding cakes are assembled and styled at your venue.
